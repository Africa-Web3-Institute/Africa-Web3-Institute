The readiness imperative

Are Ghanaian banks ready for the VASP era?

Ghana’s Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, 2025 (Act 1154) changes the operating environment for banks. As virtual assets move into the formal regulatory perimeter, banks will increasingly encounter VASPs through customer relationships, payments, settlement, compliance, liquidity and emerging financial products.

This creates new risks — and new strategic opportunities. This is an executive banking readiness programme focused on institutional preparedness, risk management and informed participation. It is not a cryptocurrency conference.

How ready is your institution to operate, compete and partner within Ghana’s regulated virtual-asset economy?

Why now?

From regulatory change to institutional strategy

Banks already possess many capabilities the emerging ecosystem needs: regulated balance sheets, trusted customer relationships, compliance infrastructure, payment connectivity, treasury expertise and institutional credibility. Those advantages do not automatically translate into digital-asset readiness.

What are we permitted to do? What risks are we exposed to? What capabilities do we need? Who should we partner with? Where should we participate — and where should we not?

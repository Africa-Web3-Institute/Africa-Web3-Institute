The opportunity

Nigeria has the assets. Now it needs the market infrastructure.

Tokenisation can widen investment access, streamline administration and open new financing channels. But putting an asset on a blockchain is the easy part.

A credible transaction must align ownership, regulation, valuation, legal structure, technology, custody, settlement, liquidity and investor protection. NRTI brings those pieces into one disciplined pathway.

Questions

Good projects begin with clarity.

Is NRTI only a training programme?

No. The Masterclass is the entry point. The initiative continues through opportunity mapping, project assessment, pilot acceleration and support towards market implementation.

What projects can be submitted?

Verifiable, productive assets such as commercial property, agricultural inventories, warehouse receipts, energy assets, infrastructure, private credit and trade receivables.

Will every submitted asset be tokenised?

No. Projects are assessed for legal readiness, commercial value, regulatory feasibility, asset quality and investor demand. Only the strongest candidates progress.

Does selection guarantee investment?

No. Selection provides structured assessment and development support; it does not guarantee investment, regulatory approval, issuance or market listing.

How can my organisation participate?

Apply for the Masterclass, submit an asset or project, or join the initiative as a Founding Partner.
